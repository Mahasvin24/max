# Max logo exploration — generation prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, one call per image. The base palette follows `max-app/DESIGN.md`: charcoal #181818 and off-white #EDEDED. Waypoint introduces a quiet sage accent for the goal dot.

The user's additional context—an AI productivity companion for achieving goals and growing together—informed Waypoint and the interpretation of the other marks. The earlier Murmur direction is preserved as a bonus alternate.

These are raster concept studies. Exact geometric repetition and path-based morphs should be constructed in SVG or SwiftUI after a direction is selected.

Asset type: original logo concept image for Max, a personal native macOS AI chat companion.

Constraints: no words, no letters unless expressly part of the requested symbol, no labels, no border, no app-icon tile, no multiple panels, no mockup, no perspective, no gradient, no shadows, no textures, no 3D, no watermark.
Primary request: Design ORBIT BLOOM, an abstract fivefold rotationally symmetric soft rotor.
Subject: exactly FIVE identical broad curved teardrop/comma petals arranged at exact 72-degree increments around the center, all sweeping clockwise. Each petal has a large rounded outer head tapering smoothly toward the center, with comfortably open curved charcoal gaps separating the petals. The five inner tips approach a small circular dark central opening. Each petal must be an exact rotated copy of the same petal, identical fill and geometry; the complete mark must map onto itself at 72 degrees. Overall silhouette is compact, gentle, almost floral yet clearly directional like a soft spinning turbine. Avoid thin sharp spikes, interwoven knots, sixfold geometry, asterisk shapes or existing AI logos. Off-white flat fill only. No face.
